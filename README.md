# China's 'Dying' Companies and Productivity Growth

## Article Synopsis
The article, published on ChinaTalk on July 28, 2026, argues that China's push for productivity-led growth _still_ faces an 
incentive constraint. In recent months, the central government has outlined a series of regulatory measures to discourage 
local officials from propping up inefficient firms. But nothing stops local leaders from circumventing those regulations. 
The Chinese bureaucracy continues to reward officials who hit GDP growth targets, minimize unemployment and social unrest, 
and defer to the dynastic mandates of President Xi Jinping. Until the underlying incentive to keep inefficient firms afloat 
is recast, China’s tireless pursuit of productivity-led growth will continue to bear little fruit. 

The link to the full article: https://www.chinatalk.media/p/chinas-dying-companies

## Code Description
There are three figures in the article - the first on per-capita GDP growth rates and incremental capital-output ratios (ICOR),
the second on total factor productivity (TFP) growth rates, and the last on yearly bankruptcy cases (accepted or concluded).
Key sources include the Penn World Table (PWT) 11.0, the annual work reports of the Supreme People's Court of China, and
